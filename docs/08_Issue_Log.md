## Latest evidence

Production run `34560417839` fetched 31/31 sources, expanded discovery to 340 leads, accepted five candidates through the concrete evidence gate and passed match-detail parity for all five. The 14-day history then retained only Steve Borthwick and Alex Usanov. Contepomi/Madigan and Gunne/McGrath were genuine repeats from 8-10 September and should remain rejected. The WXV squad announcement was a false history collision with an older generic WXV competition position because `squads` had no recognised selection phase.

The same artifact shows why reserve semantics needed correction: the final gate required eight eligible positions (five missing plus three reserve), whereas the launch contract is five publishable positions with three reserves preferred. PR #486 keeps the refill target at eight but makes five the strict minimum when no drafts are retained.

PR #486 also extends coherent identity support to player stories where the named fact-ledger person is identifiable in the title and the development has at least two independent publishers and two substantive facts. Surname-collision detection remains fail-closed, and explicit non-rugby title signals such as Nations League, soccer and GAA are rejected. No AI reservation has been made on 11 September in the completed pre-run proofs.
