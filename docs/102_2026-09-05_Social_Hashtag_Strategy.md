# Social hashtag strategy — 5 September 2026

Social publishing remains downstream of the mandatory human Sanity publish action.

The social formatter now uses a deterministic relevance-first hashtag strategy rather than a fixed generic set. Each post always includes `#TheRugbyPanda`, preserves any owner/editor supplied hashtags, derives team/competition/context tags from the article title and standfirst, and adds broad rugby discovery tags only after subject-specific tags.

Instagram is capped at eight hashtags and Facebook at four to avoid spammy over-tagging. No OpenAI call is used for hashtag selection.

Supported deterministic topics currently include Leinster, Munster, Ulster, Connacht, Ireland/Irish Rugby, All Blacks/New Zealand, Springboks/South Africa, Wallabies/Australia, England, France, Scotland, Wales, Italy, Six Nations, Champions Cup, Challenge Cup, URC, Rugby World Cup and women's rugby.

This change is designed to improve discoverability while keeping tags directly relevant to the article. It does not claim that hashtags alone guarantee reach or follower growth; page performance must still be measured from real post engagement over time.
