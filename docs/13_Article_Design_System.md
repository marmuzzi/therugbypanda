# Article Design System

The published article page must feel like a modern sports publication, not a document, book page or marketing landing page.

## Fixed principles

- Headline and site chrome use the strong sans-serif brand style.
- Article copy uses the restrained site serif (`font-serif`) at 18–20px with a comfortable line height.
- Reading measure is 720–760px and centred inside the article page.
- No drop caps.
- No decorative rule above the opening paragraph.
- No oversized paragraph gaps.
- The story begins before supplementary modules.
- Key Points are secondary and appear after the article copy, not between the headline and the story.
- Portable Text strong, emphasis, headings, quotes, links and lists must remain visible.
- Bold text must have a clearly heavier weight than surrounding copy.

These principles should not be reversed without an explicit editorial-design decision.
