# Welcome to The Rugby Panda

Independent rugby coverage, thoughtful analysis and stories from the game we all love.

If you're reading this, there's a good chance you love rugby as much as we do.

Not because every match is perfect.

Quite the opposite.

We love the Monday morning debates after a European weekend.

We love spotting the next academy player before everyone knows their name.

We love the tactical battles that never make the highlights.

And yes... we occasionally think the referee got it wrong too.

Rugby has never lacked people telling us **what** happened.

What we've always wanted is someone to explain **why**.

Why a selection mattered.

Why momentum shifted.

Why one decision changed an entire match.

That's what you'll find here.

Clear reporting.

Thoughtful analysis.

And stories that respect the intelligence of rugby supporters.

Our focus is the rugby we all care about.

**Leinster. Munster. Ulster. Connacht. The URC. The Ireland national team. And the biggest stories from the international game.**

Whether you've found us before kick-off, after the final whistle, or somewhere in between, we're delighted you're here.

**Now, let's talk rugby.**

— **The Rugby Panda Team**
